SKY GRAPPLE

Grapple across endless sky islands. Don't fall.

📲 To install on iPhone: tap Share then Add to Home Screen.
How to play
  • Shoot your hook where you aim. When it sticks, the button turns into Pull – tap it to get launched (with a climb boost). Works mid-air too.
  • Missed? Hit ✕ (right-click / Q) to reel the hook back.
  • Hook an enemy or player to damage them, then Yank to fling them toward (and past) you – right off the island!
  • Enemies come in types: Grapplers, fast swap-happy Zippers (pink), long-range Snipers (orange) big Brutes that hit hard, Bombers with exploding hooks, Magnetrons that yank everyone nearby and see-through Phantoms that teleport behind you, icy Frostbites that slow you, pouncing Leapers, Splitters that split in two, Medics that heal the others and exploding Kamikazes. About once a minute a crowned boss appears: the SKY TITAN, THUNDER KING, VOID MAW, HIVE QUEEN or INFERNO LORD. Each one drops its own rare ability when you kill it.
  • Red Grapplers walk, run, grapple between islands, hook you to them, and do the dangerous swap: they fly to you and you get flung to where they were.
  • Fall into the clouds and you respawn at the safe spawn island after 5 seconds.
  • Shops also sell abilities (Swap Shot, Super Leap, Dash, Shockwave, Rewind, Magnet, Shield, Glide, Hook Bomb, Heal) and the legendary PARRY. Each gets its own button.
  • Collect 🪙 coins and find the rare SHOP on big islands (follow the arrow at the top) for grapple upgrades.
  • Online is free-for-all with friendly fire. You are blue, other players glowing green (visible through walls), enemies red.
